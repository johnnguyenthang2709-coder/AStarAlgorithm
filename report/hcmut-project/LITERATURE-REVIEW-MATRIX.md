## Coverage and method

Recursively inventoried `reference/Papers` on 2026-10-09: **8 accessible PDFs, 7 unique studies**, no additional PDF subdirectories. Text was extracted page by page from seven files. `astarNilsson.pdf` has no usable text layer; all eight scanned pages were rendered and read visually (journal pp. 100–107; page 100 also contains the preceding article's bibliography). Both 21-page Phan files were reviewed; their extracted page text is identical, although binary hashes differ. They are cited once, not counted as independent evidence. No inaccessible paper was omitted. Metadata and claims come from the papers themselves; DOI/venue checks use publishers or author/institution pages. Full file hashes are in `literature-inventory.json`.

This is a thematic synthesis, not an implementation recommendation to add the surveyed algorithms. No provided paper is a dedicated empirical road-routing study. Hart and the supplementary Dijkstra foundation directly support graph search; geometric distance lower bounds for the actual road data are established by code and frozen edge checks.

## Synthesis and impact on interpretation

1. **Search guarantees are representation-specific.** Hart/Dijkstra support cost-optimal graph search. Theta*/geometric planning explain why sparse graphs and constrained headings change available paths. The report interprets mixed ASP/A* lengths as representation-and-validity results, not a contradiction of A*.
2. **Observation and exploration are separate from shortest paths.** Elfes/Yamauchi motivate accumulation and unknown space; TangentBug shows that escape/progress logic needs its own assumptions. Our deterministic sensor, frontier ranking, and observed ancestry remain engineering choices with conditional guarantees.
3. **Approximation, convergence, and execution differ.** Phan's DAP approximation, restricted Algorithm 3 convergence, and published-source coordinate updates are separately discussed. Neither our return bound nor a logged path demonstrates universal unknown-world completion.
4. **Performance includes preparation.** Theta* and D* Lite motivate measuring graph construction and repeated work. Our frozen cache comparison preserves behavior; unmatched Python/C++ core times cannot establish an intrinsic planner speedup.

All nine bibliography entries are cited substantively. No novelty claim, paper algorithm implementation, or production behavior was added by the literature review.
